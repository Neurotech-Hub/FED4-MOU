Fixed Assumptions

  • Engineering debt to date: $6,000 (FY26: ~100 hrs at LABOR_RATE = $60/hr).
  • Pre-production engineering: $2,400 (~40 hrs at LABOR_RATE = $60/hr) expected before the device is ready to enter the 1,000-unit agreement. Total engineering investment before sales begin: $8,400.
  • PACKAGING_COST: fixed at $20 per device (not exposed as a slider).
  • NTH_INTERNAL_MARGIN: fixed at 30%.
  • INSTITUTIONAL_GOODS_TAX: 20% on external sales (paid to WashU; Hub margin is unchanged).
  • Sales mix: assumed 50% internal / 50% external. Informational only — the Hub's per-unit profit is the same in either channel.
  • RESTOCK_BATCH: 100 units. The Hub restocks 100 at a time, starting with an initial batch before unit 1.
  • EXCLUSIVE_UNITS: 1,000 units — the end of the exclusive distribution phase modeled here.
